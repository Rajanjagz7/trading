Tutorial & How-To Guide

          
How to scan, read, and open charts for every indicator and strategy in this app.

        

      

      
    


    
    

      
      
      
      
    


    


      
      

        
What this app does

        

          This is an F&O options scanner for NSE (and SENSEX/India VIX spot) that screens every stock & index for
          Day Low / Previous Close / Open=Low / Open=High price setups, and overlays 13 algorithmic indicators
          (UT Bot, Trade Predictor, Quantum Matrix, Supertrend, ORB, VWAP+RSI and more) on live option and index charts.
        

        
        
Quick Start (3 steps)

        

          

            Step 1 — Pick a data mode.
             Use the tabs below the header: ⚡ Daily Settlement Data (auto-synced NSE EOD contracts, works even after market close), 🔴 Live Market Scanner (fetches one symbol's live option chain right now), or 📁 Manual CSV Upload (your own bhavcopy file).
          

          

            Step 2 — Run a scan or pick an indicator.
             Either set your own filters (Underlying, Day Low range, Prev Close range, etc.) and click Scan, or just click one of the 13 colored indicator buttons at the top (⚡ UT Bot, 🔮 Trade Predictor, 🌌 Quantum Matrix…) to instantly scan every symbol with that strategy's own logic pre-filled.
          

          

            Step 3 — Open the chart.
             Click any row in the results table, or any index ticker card (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, INDIA VIX, SENSEX, NIFTY MIDCAP) at the top of the page, to open that contract's live chart with your chosen indicator fitted on it — no lag, one click.
          

        

      


      
      

        
The 3 data modes

        
    
          
  • ⚡ Daily Settlement Data (NSE Auto-Sync) — loads the latest official NSE F&O settlement file automatically every trading day; best for end-of-day screening across all 200+ symbols at once.
    • 
          
  • 🔴 Live Market Scanner — pick one symbol from the dropdown (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, RELIANCE, etc.) and it pulls that symbol's real-time option chain straight from the exchange, refreshing on demand.
    • 
          
  • 📁 Manual CSV Upload — drag in your own NSE bhavcopy CSV (today's file required, previous session's optional) if you want to scan a specific saved file instead of live data.
    • 
        

        
Filter fields explained

        
    
          
  • Underlying — search or pick a specific stock/index, or leave on "All symbols" to scan everything at once.
    • 
          
  • Instrument type — include Index options, Stock options, or both.
    • 
          
  • Expiry — "All expiries" or just each symbol's "Nearest expiry".
    • 
          
  • Option type — Both / CE (Call) / PE (Put).
    • 
          
  • Day low in range (₹) + Tolerance — matches contracts whose Day Low falls in your ₹ band; tolerance controls how close Open must be to Low/High to count as an "O=L" / "O=H" signal.
    • 
          
  • Prev Close in range (₹) — matches contracts whose previous session's closing premium falls in your ₹ band.
    • 
          
  • Min OI — hides contracts with open interest below this number (filters out illiquid junk).
    • 
          
  • Show mode — "Matches only" (both range filters passed), "O=L" / "O=H" only, "Any signal", or "All" (everything that passed your hard filters, narrowed to ~21 strikes around the ATM spot for readability).
    • 
        

        
The 13 quick-scan buttons

        

          The colored buttons above the mode tabs (⚡ UT Bot, 🔮 Trade Predictor, 🌌 Quantum Matrix, 🎯 Target Proj, 📈 Supertrend, 🚀 15m ORB, 🌊 VWAP+RSI, 📊 Volume & OI, 🕯️ Candlestick, 1️⃣/2️⃣/3️⃣ Strategy 1-3, 📊 AlphaScan Matrix) each run a scan across every stock and index using that strategy's own built-in filter logic — you don't need to set the filter fields yourself. See the 📊 All 13 Indicators tab above for what each one actually looks for.
        

      


      
      

        
Click Open This Indicator on any card to jump straight into its live option chain & Pine Script v6 code.

        

      


      
      

        
Opening a chart

        
    
          
  • Ticker cards at the top of the page (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, INDIA VIX, SENSEX, NIFTY MIDCAP) — click any card to open its live spot chart with your last-used indicator already fitted.
    • 
          
  • Any scan result row — click a row (or its 📈 chart icon) in the results table to open that exact option contract's chart, fully live and lag-free.
    • 
          
  • 📊 Indicator Option Chain & Scripts button — opens the full indicator hub: a live, indicator-fitted option chain for any index/stock plus the raw Pine Script v6 code for that indicator (copy it straight into TradingView).
    • 
          
  • 📊 View Option Chain button — a plain live option chain viewer for the currently selected symbol.
    • 
        

        
Setting alerts

        

          Click 🔔 Price Alerts to create a trigger on any symbol/strike/type for a target price, stop-loss price, or a UT Bot Buy/Sell signal.
          You'll get an audio chime, a device vibration, and (if you enable 🔔 Browser Alerts) a desktop notification the moment it fires — no need to keep the tab focused.
        

        
SENSEX & India VIX note

        

          SENSEX and India VIX show real live/closing spot prices on their ticker cards and charts, but NSE has no F&O contracts for either (SENSEX options trade only on BSE, which blocks this app's requests) — so their option-chain scan tables will correctly show "no data" instead of fake numbers.
